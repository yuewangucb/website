Biography

Yue is passionate about studying romantic and post-romantic period music. She is especially interested in music influenced by different cultures around the world. In 2021, she made her concerto debut with the University of Rochester Symphony Orchestra. In the same year, she participated in a masterclass with Peter Takacs.


Besides studying and performing solo piano, Yue enjoyed performing as a collaborative pianist. She received the National Music Festival Apprentice Fellowship in 2022. From 2018 to 2021, she served as a principal pianist for the University of Rochester Orchestras under Director Rachel Waddell.
